Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 · App: Khushu (Huşu) — Prayer Times & Wudu (iOS and Android)

In short: Khushu has no accounts, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking. Your prayer log, wudu status, profile and preferences live only on your phone. The only personal data that leaves the device is your approximate location, sent to calculate prayer times and find nearby mosques. Premium purchases are handled by Apple and Google; your card details never reach us.

1. Controller and contact

This app is published by the Khushu developers Onur Demir and Furkan Durgun. For any question or request about your personal data: husu.support@gmail.com

What we do not collect

  • Accounts, e-mail addresses, phone numbers or passwords (there is no sign-up).
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics, crash reports or behavioural tracking.
  • A copy of your address book. Contacts are read only while you pick one and never leave the device.
  • Payment card details. Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

3. Services that receive your location

Your location is sent only when you use the related feature and only as latitude/longitude; no name, device identifier or other personal data is attached. These providers' own privacy policies apply.

  • Aladhan API (aladhan.com) — prayer time calculation. Sent: location, date, calculation method.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de — Germany; overpass.kumi.systems — Austria/EU) and Photon (photon.komoot.io — Germany) — finding nearby mosques. Sent: location and search radius.
  • Google Places API (Google LLC — USA) — only if enabled, as an additional source for nearby mosques. Sent: location.

Calculated prayer times are cached on your device so the app also works offline. Your location is transmitted to these services only for the current query and is never stored on any server of ours.

4. Premium purchases

The Premium subscription is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. To verify subscription status and restore it across devices we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). Data sent to RevenueCat: a randomly generated anonymous app user id, the purchase receipt/token, product id, subscription dates and the device platform. Your name, e-mail or payment details are not shared with RevenueCat or with us. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

Prayer-time, reminder, wudu and scheduled-message notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. No push server is used and no data is sent out for notifications.

6. Permissions

  • Location (while using): prayer times, Qibla and nearby mosques. If declined, you can pick a city manually.
  • Notifications: prayer-time and reminder alerts.
  • Exact alarms (Android): so notifications arrive at the exact minute.
  • Contacts: only while choosing a scheduled-message recipient, optional.
  • Motion sensors: Qibla compass only.

Every permission can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

7. Legal basis

Data is processed under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), on the basis of performance of our usage relationship with you (KVKK art. 5/2-c, GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interest in operating the app (KVKK art. 5/2-f, GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) and your consent given through device permissions (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)).

8. Retention

  • On-device data is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. Reset options on the Profile screen let you erase your records.
  • Cached prayer times are purged automatically after 45 days.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple/Google and RevenueCat for the life of the subscription and as required by law.

9. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to processing and to data portability. You have full control over on-device data: uninstalling the app deletes all local data. For any other request write to husu.support@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or, in the EU, your local supervisory authority.

10. Children

Khushu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Age is used only for the missed-prayer estimate and only on the device.

11. Security

All network connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Data is stored in your device's protected app storage. As no method is 100% secure, we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock.

12. International transfers

The providers listed in sections 3 and 4 may be located in Germany, Austria and the USA. Only anonymous location queries and anonymous purchase-verification data are sent to them.

13. Changes

When this policy is updated the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. Material changes are announced inside the app.
